# Golden ratio as a visible, explicit element in studio/brand websites, portfolios and product communication

Verification note: research date 2026-10-06. Several primary pages (fastcompany.com, goldennumber.net, uchi.co.uk) were blocked by the network proxy, so many items below come from search-result snippets and are marked **[snippet / claimed]**. Items marked **[verified-multi]** were corroborated by several independent sources in the results. Nothing was checked visually (no screenshots of overlays).

## 1. Studios (industrial design, furniture, architecture) with websites built explicitly on the golden ratio and showing it openly

### Takeaway
I found no well-known industrial-design, furniture or architecture studio whose own website uses visible golden-spiral or golden-rectangle construction lines over its product or space photography as its main idea. Where φ shows up on studio or brand sites, it is in a blog post, in the copy ("designed with the golden section") or in a hidden layout grid. It is not drawn over the photos.

### Cited Findings
- Uchi, a UK furniture/interiors company, has a blog post titled "uchi's Golden Section". This is an editorial use, not a site concept. I could not open the page — [uchi.co.uk](https://uchi.co.uk/blog/uchis-golden-section/) **[title only; content unverified]**
- Lists of award-winning architecture websites mention no golden-ratio concept. Examples are Minale + Mann (Awwwards and others), Shape Studio and Makhno Studio (Awwwards Site of the Day), and BIG (scroll in two directions). None mention φ — [Plug and Play Design, 2025](https://www.plugandplaydesign.co.uk/best-architect-websites-2025/) **[snippet]**
- Advice on golden-ratio web design is generic: a 1:1.618 column split, a type scale based on φ and a "Phi Grid" theme. It comes from agencies, theme vendors and blogs, not from named design studios showing their own work — [UX Movement](https://uxmovement.com/content/applying-the-golden-ratio-to-layouts-and-rectangles/), [Design Shack](https://designshack.net/articles/graphics/applying-the-golden-ratio-to-your-web-designs/), [Silphium Design](https://silphiumdesign.com/golden-ratio-portfolio-themes-garden-photo/) **[snippet]**
- On Dribbble, individual designers post shots like "Layout based on golden ratio grid" (Radovesely). These are concept shots, not working studio sites — [Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/radovesely/shots) **[snippet]**
- Architecture has a historic precedent that states its system openly: Le Corbusier's Modulor (developed 1943, book 1948/1950). It is a proportion scale built on the human body and the golden section (figure 1.83 m, navel 1.13 m, raised hand 2.26 m, two Fibonacci-like series). At the Unité d'Habitation (Marseille) it governs plan, section, elevations, brise-soleil and columns. A 2.26 m Modulor relief is cast into the concrete and "declares" the system on the building itself — [Wikipedia: Modulor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modulor), [ICON](https://www.iconeye.com/design/modulor-man-by-le-corbusier), [KSU math commentary PDF](https://math.ksu.edu/~rozhkovs/Modulor_oct19final.pdf) **[verified-multi]**
- Furniture with a Fibonacci-sequence system: the "Fibonacci shelf" (Lexus Design Award 2012) has six nested metal frames at 55/34/21/13/8/5 cm. It is shown on Designboom — [designboom](https://www.designboom.com/project/fibonacci-shelf/) **[snippet]**

### Inferences
- The well-known precedent for stating proportion openly is the Modulor relief on the Unité: the system is marked on the object itself. R11's on-photo annotations ("φ eye · hinge") are a digital, per-photo version of that gesture. On the evidence I found, a studio site does not commonly treat it as its core concept.
- Studio sites with "golden ratio layout" are hard to search for because the grid is not visible. It is unlikely that a famous studio uses visible overlays on its photos, because it would have been featured on Awwwards or in design press.

### Gaps
- I could not search Awwwards or Behance internally, and their search pages are not indexed well. A small or student portfolio with spiral overlays could exist on Behance (see the "fibonacci spiral" search page: [Behance](https://www.behance.net/search/projects/fibonacci%20spiral)). I could not exclude this.
- I found no named studio, URL and year for a site whose navigation or logo grid is built on φ and shown openly.

## 2. Brands and products that market themselves with golden-ratio overlays or claims (logos, cars, watches, eyewear, furniture)

### Takeaway
Golden-ratio overlays are common in **logo** communication. Pepsi's 2008/09 "Breathtaking" document by the Arnell Group is the main example, and the Twitter bird and Apple/iCloud are the main fan analyses. Physical-product brands mostly make **verbal** claims (Aston Martin, watchmakers, an eyewear label called "The Golden Ratio"). Much of the overlay material is retrofitted, third-party analysis that design critics have debunked.

### Cited Findings
- **Pepsi "Breathtaking" (2008, leaked 2009):** a 27-page document by Peter Arnell's Arnell Group about the Pepsi globe redesign. It overlays golden-ratio and "relativity" construction lines on the logo, and links it to the Mona Lisa, the Parthenon, feng shui, the Earth's magnetic field and more. It was leaked online in Feb 2009. Its authenticity was questioned at first, but it has been widely reported since then. The redesign has been reported to cost about $1M — [SlideShare copy](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/breathtaking-pepsi/1281698), [calvininjax 2009 (hoax-or-real)](https://calvininjax.wordpress.com/2009/02/11/pepsi-logo-document-a-hoax-or-the-real-thing/), [Typeroom](https://www.typeroom.eu/content/ten-years-ago-pepsi-paid-1-million-logo-redesign-why), [Fortune 2024](https://fortune.com/2024/05/19/logo-redesign-million-dollars-pepsi) **[verified-multi]**
- **Apple iCloud logo (June 2011):** Australian designer Alan van Roemburg overlaid circles and said the cloud's "puffs" follow 1:1.6. It was widely reported (MacRumors, Engadget). Critics noted that 1.6 is not φ (1.618…) and that Apple never claimed it — [MacRumors 2011](https://www.macrumors.com/2011/06/16/icloud-logo-infused-with-golden-ratio/), [Engadget 2011](https://www.engadget.com/2011-06-24-the-icloud-logo-and-the-golden-ratio.html), [Mike Afford](https://www.mikeafford.com/blog/golden-ratio-icloud-icon-revisited/) **[verified-multi; claim is third-party, not Apple's]**
- **Apple logo:** Fast Company's "Debunking the Myth of Apple's 'Golden Ratio'" and Cult of Mac / Forbes-Quora pieces say Rob Janoff drew the 1977 logo essentially freehand, so the golden-circle overlays are after the fact — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/1672682/debunking-the-myth-of-apple-s-golden-ratio), [Cult of Mac](https://www.cultofmac.com/news/was-apples-logo-really-designed-using-the-golden-ratio), [Forbes 2013](https://www.forbes.com/sites/quora/2013/05/17/does-the-apple-logo-really-adhere-to-the-golden-ratio/) **[snippet; FastCo page blocked]**
- **Twitter bird (June 2012):** designed by Martin Grasser with Doug Bowman's in-house team. The construction is shown as overlapping circles, often described as "15 circles built on the golden ratio". The circles are an official construction. The "golden ratio" part is mostly repeated by third parties — [LogoDecks on X](https://x.com/LogoDecks/status/2041410397400412314), [Designbro](https://designbro.com/blog/brand-logos/twitter-logo-saga/), [logobird](https://logobird.com/principles/golden-ratio/) **[snippet; golden-ratio attribution claimed]**
- **Critical counterpoint:** John Brownlee, "The Golden Ratio: Design's Biggest Myth", Fast Company Co.Design, 13 April 2015. It calls the aesthetic claims an urban legend and cites Markowsky's 1992 debunking of the Parthenon and similar claims. Gary Meisner (goldennumber.net) wrote a rebuttal — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/3044877/the-golden-ratio-designs-biggest-myth), [goldennumber.net rebuttal](https://www.goldennumber.net/did-fastcodesign-censor-reader-comments-on-the-golden-ratio/) **[snippet]**
- **Aston Martin (cars):** Marek Reichman, chief creative officer, says the golden ratio "sits at the heart of every modern Aston Martin". The company says the DB11 reached φ "to within 9 decimal places" in one key proportion. Reichman uses this in design masterclasses. Bowmore × Aston Martin also marketed a 21-year-old whisky "made using the golden ratio" — [Gentleman's Journal](https://www.thegentlemansjournal.com/article/aston-martins-chief-creative-officer-marek-reichman-explains-how-he-designed-the-dbx/), [OneShift masterclass](https://www.oneshift.com/articles/features/aston-martin-design-masterclass/), [Robb Report](https://robbreport.com/food-drink/spirits/bowmore-aston-martin-masters-selection-21-year-old-1234646579/), [goldennumber.net](https://www.goldennumber.net/aston-martin-golden-ratio/) **[snippet; brand's own claim]**
- **Watches:** These watches are said to follow the golden ratio: Patek Philippe Golden Ellipse (1968, explicitly marketed on golden-section proportions), A. Lange & Söhne Lange 1 (dial layout), Parmigiani Toric, JLC Reverso and Rosendahl Watch II (Flemming Bo Hansen) — [The Peak SG](https://www.thepeakmagazine.com.sg/watch-jewellery/luxury-timepieces-golden-ratio-design-inspiration), [Oracle of Time on Parmigiani](https://oracleoftime.com/parmigiani-and-the-golden-ratio/) **[snippet; Golden Ellipse brand claim well known, others partly press interpretation]**
- **Eyewear:** "The Golden Ratio" is a Philippine eyewear label that uses the concept as its brand name and story (Mazzucchelli acetate). I found no evidence of construction overlays on its product photos — [thegoldenratio.store](https://thegoldenratio.store/), [Garage PH 2021](https://garage.com.ph/2021/12/03/we-found-the-perfect-frames-from-homegrown-brand-the-golden-ratio/) **[snippet]**
- **Third-party product overlays:** Gary Meisner's goldennumber.net / PhiMatrix publishes golden-ratio grid overlays on product photos: cars, the iPod Classic, Apple's iOS icon template, and Timberland boots and Apple products according to other sources. These are the analyst's overlays, not the brands' communication. PhiMatrix also sells "golden ratio design services" — [goldennumber.net product design](https://www.goldennumber.net/product-design/), [PhiMatrix](https://www.phimatrix.com/), [services](https://www.phimatrix.com/golden-ratio-design-services/) **[snippet]**
- A 2015 ResearchGate paper, "Applicability of golden ratio rule in modern product design", analyzes industrial products with golden-ratio overlays (academic, not marketing) — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/281584787_Aplicability_of_golden_ratio_rule_in_modern_product_design) **[snippet]**

### Inferences
- Agency or analyst overlays on **logos** are an established genre. Overlays on **3D product or space photos** mostly come from fans or analysts (Meisner, Pinterest, blogs). They are rare as a brand's own communication. Aston Martin says it verbally but did not appear to publish spiral overlays itself.
- Because of Pepsi "Breathtaking", golden-ratio overlays carry a known risk in design culture: they read as pseudo-justification. R11 would do well to present its overlays with self-awareness or as a crop-composition device, not as proof of beauty.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether Aston Martin's official media or website shows spiral or grid overlays on car photos, or only states the claim in words.
- I did not check Apple's iOS icon grid (a circles-and-squares template that third parties label "golden").

## 3. Photography and portfolio tools and social trends that overlay Fibonacci spirals on photos

### Takeaway
Spiral overlays on photos are a mainstream, commodity tool. Lightroom and Photoshop have them built in, and many web tools and apps offer them. The "golden ratio face" trend on TikTok and in cosmetic surgery PR is also widespread. Putting a spiral on a photo is therefore not new in itself.

### Cited Findings
- Adobe Lightroom Classic crop tool: press O to cycle the Crop Guide Overlays (Thirds, Golden Ratio, Golden Spiral, Diagonals and more). Shift+O rotates or flips the spiral, giving 8 orientations — [Have Camera Will Travel](https://havecamerawilltravel.com/lightroom/crop-overlays/), [Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/lightroom-classic-discussions/crop-guide-overlay-the-golden-spiral/td-p/8796064), [Amateur Photographer](https://amateurphotographer.com/technique/get-crop-tool-lightroom/) **[verified-multi]**
- Web and app tools that overlay the phi grid (38.2/61.8), the golden spiral or golden triangles on uploaded photos and export them:
  - GridMakerPro: [golden ratio](https://gridmakerpro.com/tools/golden-ratio/), [spiral](https://gridmakerpro.com/tools/fibonacci-spiral/)
  - Phiora (Michelle J. Feldman; includes live camera view): [phiora.app](https://phiora.app/)
  - Imagen AI: [cropping tool](https://imagen-ai.com/tools/golden-ratio-cropping-tool/)
  - PhiMatrix desktop software (Gary Meisner): [phimatrix.com](https://www.phimatrix.com/)

  **[snippet]**
- Composition guides tell photographers to place the subject at the spiral's "eye" — [Photzy](https://photzy.com/getting-creative-with-the-fibonacci-spiral/), [ExpertPhotography](https://expertphotography.com/golden-ratio-photography) **[snippet]**
- "Golden ratio face": London surgeon Dr Julian De Silva publishes yearly celebrity rankings based on φ mapping (e.g., Emma Stone first in 2025 at 94.72%, as reported). TikTok has viral "Golden Ratio" face filters with mask overlays — [TikTok discover](https://www.tiktok.com/discover/golden-ratio-mask-filter), [WPR newsletter](https://wpr-newsletter.beehiiv.com/p/the-world-s-most-symmetrical-faces), [AOL](https://www.aol.com/reg-jean-page-michael-b-190627532.html) **[snippet]**

### Inferences
- Viewers have seen the spiral-on-photo look many times. R11's originality cannot come from the overlay itself. It has to come from something else: the per-product crop rule (spiral eye placed on a named part such as a hinge), the technical annotations, and the treatment as a whole-site system.

### Gaps
- I did not measure how common Instagram hashtag posts with spiral-overlaid product shots are.

## 4. Studio sites that combine golden-ratio photo overlays with annotations as their core concept

### Takeaway
Within the limits of this search, I found **no** studio or brand site whose core concept is product or space photography cropped to the golden spiral, with overlay annotations naming the part at the spiral's eye (like "φ eye · hinge" or "side φ⁻ⁿ"). The nearest precedents are elsewhere: Pepsi's annotated logo document (print or PDF, a logo, ridiculed), Meisner/PhiMatrix analyst overlays on products (third-party, analytical), Le Corbusier's Modulor relief (an annotated proportion system on a building), and generic overlay tools.

### Cited Findings
- Searches for interactive sites with spiral overlays that can be toggled on photos with annotations returned only tools, not studio sites: GridMakerPro, Phiora, Imagen and PhiMatrix — [Phiora](https://phiora.app/), [GridMakerPro](https://gridmakerpro.com/tools/fibonacci-spiral/), [PhiMatrix](https://www.phimatrix.com/) **[snippet]**
- Lists of architecture and studio websites (2024–2025) show no φ-overlay concept — [Plug and Play Design](https://www.plugandplaydesign.co.uk/best-architect-websites-2025/), [Pixpa](https://www.pixpa.com/blog/architecture-portfolio-websites), [Minimalio](https://minimalio.org/architecture-interior-designer-portfolio-websites-examples/) **[snippet]**
- Annotated construction overlays exist for logos (Pepsi Breathtaking, Twitter circles) and in analyst diagrams of products (PhiMatrix). I found none that a studio uses as a live site system on its own portfolio photos — see sources in section 2.

### Inferences
- R11's concept looks original as a full-site system. It joins three known parts that I found nowhere together on a studio site: (a) the crop rule of a photo tool (Lightroom), (b) the annotation style of a construction document (Pepsi- or Modulor-style labels), and (c) the eye placed on a named functional part of the product.
- To justify it, the site should acknowledge the lineage: Modulor, Lightroom overlays, and the debate in Fast Company 2015. It should also present φ as an editorial cropping rule, not as a claim about beauty, so it does not get read as "Breathtaking".

### Gaps
- Absence of evidence is not proof. Small portfolios (Behance, Cargo, Read.cv, student sites) and non-English studios (Japanese, Korean, Italian) were not searched systematically. Before claiming originality publicly, search Behance, Cargo and Instagram manually for "golden spiral product photography" and "φ crop".
